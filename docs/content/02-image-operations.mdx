---
title: Image operations
description: Resize, crop, rotate, flip, and adjust images.
---

Each operation returns a lazy [pipeline](/pipelines) that you can chain and
finish with `encode`, `saveToFile`, or `toImage`. The original image is never
changed.

## Resize

```dart
final fitted = image.resize(800, 600).toImage();
final exact = image.resizeExact(800, 600).toImage();
```

`resize` preserves the aspect ratio. `resizeExact` may stretch or squash the
image.

Choose a resize filter when needed:

```dart
final pixels = image
    .resize(320, 240, filter: FilterTypeEnum.Nearest)
    .encode(const PixerPngEncoder());
```

The available filters are `Nearest`, `Triangle`, `CatmullRom`, `Gaussian`, and
`Lanczos3`. `Lanczos3` is the default.

## Crop

```dart
image.crop(100, 100, 400, 300).saveToFile('cropped.png');
```

The arguments are `x`, `y`, `width`, and `height`. The crop rectangle must fit
inside the image produced by the preceding operations.

## Rotate and flip

```dart
image.rotate90();
image.rotate180();
image.rotate270();
image.flipHorizontal();
image.flipVertical();
```

## Adjust

```dart
image.blur(2.5);
image.brightness(30);
image.contrast(20);
image.grayscale();
image.invert();
```

Operations chain in any order, for example
`image.resize(800, 600).contrast(10).grayscale()`. Dispose every image returned
by `toImage` after use.
